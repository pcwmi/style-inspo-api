# User Research: Kate & Dana Group Thread
**Date:** Jan 31 - Feb 4, 2026
**Context:** Group iMessage thread, both are style enthusiasts and product friends

## Participants
- **Kate** (+1 415 471-9099) - Excited first-time user, gets decision paralysis getting dressed
- **Dana** (+1 925 451-8511) - Power user, already uses ChatGPT for styling, has 300+ outfit photos, technical (builds iOS apps)

---

## Key Insight #1: The "De-risk Getting Dressed" Use Case

**Kate's problem statement:**
> "For me the problem statement / use case is - I'd rather spend like 10-15 mins exploring outfit options on my computer/phone and get 1-2 candidates to a place with high confidence, then try them on to confirm. Vs current - spend 30 minutes trying random shit on in my closet decide I hate everything end up with like a hodge podge of whatever best I could come up with before my boyfriend is like dude we're 15 mins late we gotta go"

**The insight:** Kate wants to **de-risk and reduce time** spent on the physical trying-on stage. The app's value isn't generating outfits—it's giving her confidence BEFORE she opens the closet.

**UX implication:**
> "The 'plan my outfit' loop is great but then 'save' felt like confirming it was worth saving which I wouldn't know until I tried it on"

She wanted to **see the outfit on a model BEFORE deciding to save it**—not after.

---

## Key Insight #2: Dana's ChatGPT Has "Taste" — Yours Doesn't (Yet)

**Dana's workflow with ChatGPT:**
1. Give it 10 styled photos of a sweater → ask to rank with pros/cons
2. Give it one photo → "how would you change this?"
3. Now: give it a picture of an item → get 3 styling options → show it her attempts → get feedback

**The magic moment:**
> "my chatgpt knows me. it'll be like 'this is very you' and it'll say 'not the most dana version of this'"

**Why it's sticky:**
> "that's what makes their product sticky"

**What Dana wants from your app:**
> "yeah this is what I need at this stage. like at first I needed alta cuz I didn't even know what went with what. but now that I have the basics I need the fine tuning help. so definitely different users"

**Implication:** Dana has trained ChatGPT on her taste over time. Your app needs a taste-learning loop.

## Key Insight #3: Words Are Hard, Visuals Are Better

**Kate on the three-word onboarding:**
> "I was so mystified by the 'describe your style' words. I was like I don't know!! Haha"
> "I felt kind of stuck with words"

**Dana's suggestion:**
> "maybe like a visual step of different style options that you choose from (kind of like interior design software where it's like tell us about your style) and you choose images"
> "I like more visual and connection stuff vs having to describe with words"

**Kate:**
> "I didn't know my style until chatgpt was kind of like preppy classic with some casual french girl and I was like oh interesting thanks for giving me the words to explain"

**Implication:** Let users pick images, then YOU give them the words.

---

## Key Insight #4: Upload Friction is Real

**Dana has 300 outfit photos but can't use them:**
> "I have a folder on my phone for all acceptable outfits, but based on this understanding I couldn't like upload my outfits and have it pull out all the different things I have in my closet, could I?"

**Feature requests:**
- Pinterest API: "if you could connect to my pinterest you'd have a great idea of my style"
- Alta export: wondered if there's a way to import from Alta
- Screenshot parsing: "if the screenshots had the data showing (like on the item screen can extract)"

**Dana noticed:**
> "adding clothing images in the dashboard was 10x faster than in the onboarding experience for some reason?"

---

## Key Insight #5: "I Need a Fashion Therapist"

**Kate's onboarding wish:**
> "I also wish in onboarding it was more free form text chat like... What do you struggle with when putting outfits together? What scenarios do you need outfits for? How do you feel about your current closet items?"
> "I think I need a fashion therapist and getting me to share would help build trust - even if you don't do much of it to start"

**Implication:** The onboarding could be conversational, building rapport and extracting styling struggles.
