## Synthesis

### What's the actual value?
- Work outfit planning (functional utility)
- Discovering 1-2 outfits she hadn't considered (surprise/novelty)
- "Buy smart" feature - understanding purchase fit

### What's blocking regular use?
- **Timing mismatch**: Morning is when she needs it, but too rushed
- **Access friction**: Website feels heavyweight vs. quick check
- **No trigger**: Nothing prompts her to think about it

### How does she think about it?
- Useful tool, not essential
- Sees potential but hasn't integrated into routine

### What would make it more useful?
- Push notification in evening (8:30pm)
- Text/chat interface for quick access
- Pinterest integration for style inspiration
