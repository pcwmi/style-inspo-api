## Categorized Feedback

### 1. WHAT'S WORKING WELL

- **Quote:** "The AI model looks cool!"

### 2. KNOWN ISSUES / BUGS

#### Model Visualization - Garment Accuracy (Low Severity)
- **Issue:** "It doesn't quite understand the clothing pieces"
- **User expectation:** "I wouldn't expect that unless we did like a 360 scan of every item"
- **Specific examples:**
  - Shorts appear as short shorts/cutoffs
  - Short sleeve white blouse shows as longsleeve
  - Sweatshirt and tanktop combo combined into one piece
- **User tolerance:** HIGH - called it "cool" despite inaccuracies

#### Complete My Look - Color Coordination (High Severity)
- **Quote:** "I picked out jeans and a shirt and hit 'complete my outfit' and the poor system had trouble with colors going together :/"
- **Pattern:** Consistent with Jan 20 feedback

#### Complete My Look - Garment Layering Logic (High Severity)
- **Quote:** "Then layered two raglans on top of each other"
- **Pattern:** REPEATED issue from Jan 20 (baggy sweatshirt under tight jacket)

## Key Insight: Visual Features Drive Engagement

**The critical pattern from this session:**

```
Homepage visuals (editorial aesthetic)
    → Inspired Alexi to return
        → Tried model visualization
            → Got excited ("mannequin in a store")
                → Attempted Complete My Look
                    → Blocked by styling logic failures
```

**Visual features are doing their job:** Creating emotional engagement and driving feature usage.

**Core styling logic is the bottleneck:** Same issues from Jan 20 persist (colors, layering).

## Actionable Takeaways

1. **Homepage redesign validated** - drove re-engagement
2. **Model visualization creates "mannequin effect"** - right emotional positioning
3. **Complete My Look still broken** - color + layering logic needs work
4. **Personal photo feature requested** - Alexi willing to test
