## Synthesis

### What's the actual value she gets?
- **NOT complete outfit generation** (the core feature)
- **Organization/discovery** - reminding her of items she forgot she owns
- **Single-item inspiration** - one piece sparks her own styling

### Why didn't she save more outfits?
- The outfits don't work physically (layering, tucking, proportions)
- AI ignores her constraints ("no skirts")
- Style output feels forced/try-hard ("sophisticated touch")
- Weather mismatch (summer outfits in winter)

### The Fundamental Gap
She uploaded 100+ items expecting **complete, wearable outfits**.
She got **physically impossible combinations** that ignore real-world garment logic.

The AI doesn't understand:
- How fabrics behave (ruffles can't tuck cleanly)
- Layering physics (baggy under tight doesn't work)
- Proportions (oversized + cropped = wrong)
- Her explicit constraints ("no skirts" ignored)

### What would make her use it more?
- Fix the garment logic (biggest issue)
- Respect user constraints
- Weather-aware suggestions
- "Complete my look" flow (start with anchor piece)
